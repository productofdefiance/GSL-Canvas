# Canvas v51.17: Dark-mode fixes

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Goal (one line):** make the Spider Career layer and every selected League chip readable in dark mode. Nothing else changes.
- **Branch (optional):** the session's own branch
- **Pull request title:** `Canvas v51.17: Dark-mode fixes`

## 1. Context

A phone audit on 17 Oct (Round 0) found two dark-mode defects on the League page that also show on desktop. League is locked on desktop, so these are fixes, not design changes. Afterwards, dark mode looks exactly as it does now, except that the Spider Career layer and the selected chips become readable.

## 2. Scope

- **File:** `src/LeagueTabs.js` (Points, Home vs Away, Spider Matrix). Touch `src/League.js` only if the same pattern turns up inside `main`, and say so.
- **Screen sizes:** desktop 1440 and phone 390, light and dark. Tablet not in scope.

## 3. What to build

No mock images: both items are colour fixes, and the numbers below are the spec.

1. **The Spider Career layer follows the ink token.**
    - Today: `LeagueTabs.js` sets `const INK = '#0f172a'` for the Career layer, and it never swaps. In dark mode the card surface is also `#0f172a`, so the Career radar line and dots, scorecard bars, legend swatch, the layer card's line swatch and its top accent strip all disappear.
    - Required: the Career layer colour is the Design System's `ink` token: light `#0f172a`, dark `#f1f5f9`. Light mode is unchanged.
    - How is Code's choice. One option: the `--c-slate-900` variable in `public/index.html` already swaps to `241 245 249` in dark mode.
    - Guard: anything that reads the layer colour as a hex string (clash checks against opponent colours, mixing, alpha suffixes) must keep working. List every use of `INK` and of the layer `color`, and say in the pull request what you did for each.
2. **Selected chips stay readable in dark mode.**
    - Today: selected controls styled `bg-slate-900 text-white` become white text (`rgb(255,255,255)`) on a `#f1f5f9` fill in dark mode. Measured on Points (Pace season strip, "All") and Spider (selected quick pick), at 390 and 1440.
    - Required: light mode unchanged (`#0f172a` fill, near-white text). Dark mode: `#f1f5f9` fill, text `#1e293b` or darker, contrast 7:1 or better.
    - Suggested: on these elements, `text-white` → `text-slate-50` (`--c-slate-50` swaps to `30 41 59` in dark mode).
    - Apply to every place in `LeagueTabs.js` that pairs `bg-slate-900` with `text-white`. Today there are five, around lines 959, 1170, 1415, 1441 and 1517: the Points season strip All / None, the Home vs Away combine-season chips, two in the Spider layer picker, and the Spider quick picks.
    - Leave alone: anything in `ui.js` marked `gsl-keep` (stays light by design) and the version badge in `League.js` (`SHOW_VERSION_TAG`).

## 4. Rules that apply

- **Dark mode:** an in-canvas switch, not the device setting. A page turns dark only once converted; League is converted.
- **Colour:** colour highlights rather than decorates; numbers stay in ink. Ink is `#0f172a` light, `#f1f5f9` dark.
- **R12:** "us" cards (the managed club, its seasons and career) get a top accent strip. The Career card's strip must be visible in both modes.
- **Club-agnostic:** no club names in code; every page renders an empty save.

## 5. Must not change

- Light mode looks identical on every League tab.
- No layout, size, spacing, copy or font change anywhere.
- Spider Matrix: Versus and Group modes, the layer picker and its search, swap, quick picks, hover highlighting, Group lines / table, Highs & lows.
- Points: Focus / Compare / Pace, the season strip and All / None, the docked hover card on touch.
- Home vs Away: select and combine seasons, the Venue ledger.
- Every table still sorts.
- Cups, Pyramid and Squad untouched.
