# Canvas v51.18: Quick fixes

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Goal (one line):** eleven small, decided fixes from the phone audit and the user's notes; no new design.
- **Branch (optional):** the session's own branch
- **Pull request title:** `Canvas v51.18: Quick fixes`

## 1. Context

The 17 Oct phone audit of Points, Home vs Away and Spider Matrix produced a list of fixes. These eleven are decided and need no mock. Bigger phone changes (benchmarks scrub, Pace controls, Venue ledger, side-by-side, Spider Group, section [i] buttons, 44px tap areas) come in later builds. Afterwards the user should see Outfit page titles, no Lab controls, and the listed League details fixed.

## 2. Scope

- **Files:** `src/ui.js`, `src/App.js`, `src/LeagueTabs.js`, `src/League.js` (minus signs on Seasons / Matchdays / Opponents), `public/index.html` only if the font defaults live there.
- **Pages:** fonts and the Lab bar affect every page. Everything else is League only.
- **Screen sizes:** phone 390 and desktop 1440, light and dark. "Phones" below means widths under 640px.

## 3. What to build

No mock images: every item is specified in numbers or exact wording.

**Canvas-wide**

1. **Fonts locked; page titles in Outfit.** The locked set is: text Lexend, page titles Outfit, big numbers Outfit at +0.02em, small numbers Atkinson Hyperlegible Next. Today `LAB_CHOICES.title.def` in `ui.js` is `'lexend'`; page titles must render in Outfit on every page. The locked values are applied to `<html>` on every load, whether or not the Lab bar is shown, and any saved `gsl-fonts` value in localStorage is ignored while the Lab is hidden (a device that saved `{"title":"lexend"}` still shows Outfit).
2. **Lab bar hidden, code kept.** Add one switch (e.g. `SHOW_LAB = false`). While false, `LabBar` is not rendered at all: no drawer, no button, no empty space above the footer. Keep `LabBar`, `LAB_CHOICES` and the CSS so that setting the switch to true brings the Lab back with no other change.
3. **True minus signs.** Every negative number on the League page uses the minus sign U+2212 (−), not the keyboard hyphen (-): goal differences, deltas, pace values, hover cards, SVG axis labels (the Pace y-axis shows -10 and -20 today). Positive numbers keep "+"; zero has no sign. Route them through one shared formatter in `ui.js` (reuse one if it exists). En dashes in scores (2 – 1) and ranges (75–99%) are untouched. Other pages get this when they are converted, unless they already use the shared `ui.js` pieces.

**Points**

4. **Best season card.** "2027–28 · Div 1" sits on one line, below the Invincible chip, at 390px. It must not wrap: at 390 the card's inner width is about 140px and the line is about 95px at 12px.
5. **Perfect matchdays card.** The list is never cut off with "…". It wraps to at most two lines. If it still doesn't fit, show as many as fit and end with "+N" (e.g. "MD 10 · 16 · 34 +4").
6. **Heat-strip legend.** "Under 25%" becomes "<25%" (the `HEAT` array). At 390 the five legend items sit on one line, in both modes.
7. **Focused season in view.** On load, and whenever the focused season changes, the season strip scrolls sideways so the focused cell is fully visible, 16px in from the strip's right edge if it was off screen. Scroll only the strip, never the page.
8. **Points race on phones: full width, bare matchday numbers.** On phones the plot uses the card's full inner width. The right margin is only as wide as the longest end label plus 6px (about 28px; today about 90px). The x-axis labels are numbers only ("1  10  20  30  38  46"); desktop keeps "MD 1" etc. Tick values still come from the data. Applies to Focus, Compare and Pace.

**Home vs Away**

9. **Highlights bar order on phones (R7).** The Points card comes first and spans the full width; Win rate and PPG, then Goal difference and Clean sheets, follow in pairs. Desktop unchanged.
10. **Swing bar, away figures in dark mode.** The points and share printed on the away (sky) segment use exactly the same colours in dark mode as in light mode (dark text on sky). Today they turn pale and low-contrast in dark mode.

**Spider Matrix**

11. **Radar labels inside the card on phones, and two copy cuts.**
    - On phones every pillar label sits fully inside the card's inner box, at least 8px from its edges, in Versus and Group. Two-word labels may wrap to two lines ("Attacking / output", "Defensive / steel", "Clean / sheets", "Blowout / rate"). Labels stay at 11px or larger; the web shrinks to the space left. Desktop unchanged.
    - Delete the Versus section subtitle "The shape on the left; every pillar on its own fixed scale on the right."
    - Group: the count line drops " · hover a chip to highlight it" ("8 of 8" stays); the section subtitle becomes "Outlines only."

## 4. Rules that apply

- **Type (locked):** Lexend for text, section and card titles; Outfit for page titles and big numbers (800, +0.02em); Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for small numbers.
- **R4:** nothing under 11px, SVG text included (one logged exception: spine option E label, 10px).
- **R7:** with an odd number of Highlights cards, the one that goes full width on phones comes first.
- **R6:** no redundancy.
- **Screen size:** `useScreen()` or Tailwind prefixes, never `window.innerWidth`.
- **No typed-in counts:** ticks, seasons and matchdays come from the data.
- **Club-agnostic:** no club names in code; every page renders an empty save.

## 5. Must not change

- Desktop layouts on every page: the only desktop differences allowed are page titles in Outfit, minus signs, and the Lab bar gone.
- All interactions: Points race modes, division filter, season strip and All / None, the docked hover card on touch; Matchday benchmarks filters, drawers and sorting; Home vs Away select and combine seasons; Spider picker, swap, quick picks, Group lines / table, Highs & lows.
- v51.17's dark-mode fixes (Career layer and selected chips).
- Light mode colours everywhere.
- Cups, Pyramid and Squad, apart from the font set and the Lab bar.

    3. No hyphen directly before a digit in League's visible text or SVG text at 390 and 1440, in all seven tabs (regex `(^|[\s(])-\d`).
    4. Points at 390, on load: the S8 cell sits fully inside the strip's visible box. Changing focus to S1 then back scrolls it into view; the page's vertical scroll position doesn't move.
    5. Points race at 390, all three modes: the plot's right edge is within 34px of the card's inner right edge; x labels contain no "MD". At 1440 they still read "MD 1" etc.
