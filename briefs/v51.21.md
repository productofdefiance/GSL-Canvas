# Canvas v51.21: Spider Matrix on Phones

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Goal (one line):** Spider Matrix reads and taps well on a phone: quick picks on one row, verdict chips, a ranked card for Group, and the tab's [i].
- **Start:** a new branch from an up-to-date main (v51.20 merged), e.g. `claude/v51-21-spider`.
- **Pull request title:** `Canvas v51.21: Spider Matrix on phones`

## 1. Context

The last build of the League phone pass. On a 390px phone, Spider's quick picks wrap to three rows; the phone scorecard has lost the desktop's verdict column; and in Group, eight seasons crowd onto each pillar's line, so ten dots sit exactly on top of others and can't be tapped (logged in v51.19). The user approved the phone designs on 17 Oct. Radar labels already sit inside the card (v51.18). The tab also gets its section [i] (R14).

Switch widths and the hierarchy of switches stay as they are: that belongs to the League standards review.

## 2. Scope

- **Files:** `src/LeagueTabs.js` (Spider), `src/ui.js` only if a shared piece needs it, `src/App.js` (version line).
- **Tab:** League → Spider Matrix (Versus and Group, seasons and clubs).
- **Screen sizes:** phone 390 and desktop 1440, light and dark. "Phones" means widths under 640px. Desktop changes only where stated.

## 3. What to build

### 3a. Versus on phones (under 640px; desktop unchanged)

1. **Quick picks on one row.** An eyebrow "Quick picks" above, then every quick pick on a single line that scrolls sideways (no wrapping), starting at the card's padding edge. Chips 36px tall with a 44px tap area, chip style and selected style as today. Every quick pick stays; none is cut. On load, and when the pairing changes, the selected chip is scrolled into view (same rules as the Points season strip: scroll only the row, never the page; stop auto-scrolling once the user scrolls it).
2. **Verdict chip on each scorecard pillar.** On the right of the pillar's header line (same line as the pillar name, above the two values):
   - Text: exactly the desktop verdict column's wording ("S4 by 6.9%", "Career by 5.2%", "S4 by 0.05"), and "Level" for a tie. Same logic, including lower-is-better pillars.
   - Style: 11px, 700, small-number font; padding 3px 7px; radius 6px; text in the leading layer's colour on a 10% tint of that colour (15% in dark mode). "Level": slate-600 on slate-100 (dark-mode tokens in dark).
   - Contrast at least 4.5:1 in both modes; where a layer colour can't reach it (e.g. a dark club colour on the dark card), fall back to ink text on the tint.
   - It takes the place of the desktop verdict column on phones only.

### 3b. Group on phones: tap a pillar to rank

3. **The pillar row is the tap target** in Lines view on phones (seasons and clubs). The whole row (name, sub-line and dot line) gets a "Tap to rank ›" hint on the right (12px, slate-500) and opens the ranked card. Individual dots are no longer tap targets on phones; on desktop they keep their hover cards as today. The tapped row gets a page-accent 10% tint and a 3px accent bar on its left while its card is open.
4. **The ranked card** is the docked bottom card (shared helper: tap another pillar to switch, tap elsewhere or scroll to close):
   - Header: the pillar name (15px, 800) left; "<n> seasons · tap one to bring it forward" (or "clubs") right, 12px slate-500.
   - One row per layer, best first, 30px tall: rank (equal values share it, shown "=1"), the layer's colour dot (12px), its label ("S4"; for clubs the drawn flag plus abbreviation), a bar on the pillar's fixed scale (6px, the layer's colour), the value (13px, 700, small-number font, same format as the scorecard).
   - "Best" follows the pillar: for Defensive steel, fewest conceded ranks first.
   - The career average sits as a divider row at its place in the order: a 2px ink tick, "Career average · 74.7%" (12px slate-500), from the data.
   - Tapping a layer row brings that layer forward everywhere (as tapping its chip does today) and keeps the card open.
   - If the list is taller than 60% of the screen, it scrolls inside the card.

### 3c. Spider [i]

5. **Three sections get the [i]:** the comparison (its title changes with the layers, e.g. "Career vs S4"), Group ("Your seasons, side by side" or "Your record against each club": one text for both) and Highs & lows. Copy the text exactly from the "Spider Matrix" part of the GSL Canvas: Section [i] Blurbs doc (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7f1a307e-587e-4990-83b2-84692fb7d5b5); drop the *(coming)* marker in Group, because this build delivers it.
6. **Remove the visible subtitles:** Group's "Outlines only." and both Highs & lows lines ("The best and worst opponent …", "Your best and worst season …").

### 3d. Tap targets

7. Every tappable thing on Spider is at least 44 × 44px on phones: Versus / Group, Change ▾ on both cards, ⇄, quick picks, the layer picker's rows and chips, Seasons / Clubs, Lines / Table, Group chips and their ✕, pillar rows, By club / By season, the minimum-games filter, the [i]. Small controls keep their look and get an invisible larger tap area (the v51.19 pattern); nothing overlaps. List any control that can't get there, and why.

## 4. Rules that apply

- **Hover cards on touch:** the shared `ui.js` helpers only; open on a tap, switch on another tap, close on a tap anywhere else or on scroll; one overlay at a time.
- **R14:** an [i] on every section; no description under section titles. **R4:** nothing under 11px. **R12:** "us" cards keep their top accent strip.
- **Flags** drawn (`ui.js` Flag), never emoji. **Minus signs** U+2212. **Curly apostrophes** (’).
- **No typed-in counts:** seasons, clubs, the career average and every value come from the data.
- **Screen size:** `useScreen()` or Tailwind prefixes, never `window.innerWidth`. **Club-agnostic:** any save renders, including an empty one.

## 5. Must not change

- Spider on desktop: layout, sizes, colours, the verdict column, dot hover cards, the radar (only the subtitles move into the [i]).
- Behaviour everywhere: the layer picker and its search, swap, quick picks, Versus / Group, Seasons / Clubs, adding and removing Group layers (up to the current limit), Lines / Table, bringing a layer forward, Highs & lows filters.
- v51.17–v51.20: the Career layer in dark mode, readable selected chips, radar labels inside the card on phones, the clash check.
- Every other tab and page.
