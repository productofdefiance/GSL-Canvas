# Canvas v51.20: Points on Phones

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Goal (one line):** Points reads well on a phone: the heat strip becomes the phone reader of Matchday benchmarks, Pace needs fewer rows, and both sections get their [i].
- **Start:** after PR #5 (v51.19) is merged, on a new branch from an up-to-date main, e.g. `claude/v51-20-points`.
- **Pull request title:** `Canvas v51.20: Points on phones`

## 1. Context

The second build of the League phone pass. On a 390px phone the Matchday benchmarks ledger is 1,100px wide and about 2,900px tall: two thirds of the tab. Pace stacks about 380px of controls before its chart. The user approved the phone designs on 17 Oct (benchmarks round 2; Pace option A). The tab also gets its section [i] (R14), the shared piece built in v51.19. A few follow-ups from v51.19 ride along (3d).

Switch widths and the hierarchy of several switches in one section are **not** part of this build: they belong to the League standards review. Leave every switch at today's width.

## 2. Scope

- **Files:** `src/LeagueTabs.js` (Points), `src/ui.js` only if the [i] icon fix (3d) or a shared scrub needs it, `src/App.js` (version line), `CLAUDE.md`, `briefs/TEMPLATE.md`, `test/` (tap test modes).
- **Tab:** League → Points. The v51.19 follow-ups touch Home vs Away's [i] text too.
- **Screen sizes:** phone 390 and desktop 1440, light and dark. "Phones" means widths under 640px.

## 3. What to build

### 3a. Matchday benchmarks on phones (under 640px; desktop unchanged)

1. **Ledger folded by default.** Below the heat strip, a full-width button "Show all 46 matchdays ▾" (the number from the data, never typed), 44px tall, 1px slate-200 border, radius 10px, 13px / 600 slate-700. Open, it reads "Hide matchdays ▴" and sits above the ledger. The ledger itself is unchanged inside (it still scrolls sideways within its box). Filters (All seasons / Div 1 / Lower divisions) and the stat cards stay above the strip, as today.
2. **Hint under the strip** (only while no card is open): "Drag across the strip to read any matchday." (12px, slate-500).
3. **The strip scrubs.** A sideways finger slide along the strip updates the card to whichever cell is under the finger (the shared scrub pattern the Points race uses); a tap opens or switches a cell's card; tap elsewhere or scroll closes it (the v51.19 shared helper). The cell under the card gets a 2px ink outline with a 2px gap, and while a card is open the other cells drop to 35% opacity. Up-and-down swipes still scroll the page.
4. **The card on phones** is the docked bottom card. Content, top to bottom:
   - Header: "MD 23" (15px, 800) left; "8 games · S1–S8" (12px, slate-500) right, from the data.
   - Four columns: **Record** (W • D • L as elsewhere), **Win %** as a filled tag (14px, 700, padding 1px 7px, radius 6px; background = that cell's heat colour; text ink #0f172a, but white on the 100% band #065f46; the same in dark mode), **PPG**, **GD** (true minus). Column labels in eyebrow style.
   - Today's rank line stays ("Your strongest matchday", "12th best of 46" …), 12px slate-500, under the columns.
   - A divider, then **Best win**: the drawn flag (`ui.js` Flag, never an emoji), the club's 3-letter abbreviation (from the club registry, e.g. TOT), the score, "· S3"; values from the same source as the ledger's Best win column. No win on that matchday: "No wins yet".
   - Right of Best win: **"See in ledger →"** (12px, 700, page accent), with a 44px-tall tap area.
5. **"See in ledger →"** unfolds the ledger if folded, closes the card, scrolls so that matchday's row sits in the middle of the screen, and marks it: page-accent 10% tint background and a 3px accent bar on its left edge. The mark stays until another row is opened, the ledger is folded, or the filter changes. It does not open the row's drawer; tapping the row still does.
6. **Desktop:** the strip and ledger stay as today (ledger open, hover card under the strip, a click on a cell opens that row). Optional only if trivial: the same card content on desktop; no "See in ledger" there.

### 3b. Pace controls on phones (option A; desktop unchanged)

7. **Three short rows above the season strip:** the mode switch (Focus / Compare / Pace), then the baseline switch, then the division chips. The "Measured against" label goes on phones.
8. **Baseline switch labels without numbers:** "Average", "Record", "Last season", on one line at 390 (no wrapping).
9. **The value moves into the legend:** the chosen baseline's dashed swatch and "Average · 2.40 PPG" (or "Record · 2.63 PPG", "Last season · 2.61 PPG (S7)"), values from the data; the record line's legend entry stays as today. Legend text 12px slate-500 with the value in 700 ink.
10. **Switch widths stay as today** (the standards review decides them).

### 3c. Points [i]

11. **Points race** and **Matchday benchmarks** get the section [i]. Copy the text exactly from the "Points" part of the GSL Canvas: Section [i] Blurbs doc (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7f1a307e-587e-4990-83b2-84692fb7d5b5; apostrophes are curly ’ there now). Drop the *(coming)* marker in Matchday benchmarks: this build delivers it.
12. **Remove both visible subtitles**, including Points race's changing line ("Season 8, point by point.", "Every season against your …"). The highlighted season in the strip and the Pace legend now carry that information.

### 3d. v51.19 follow-ups

13. **Home vs Away [i] text:** straight apostrophes become ’ (the blurbs doc is corrected), so the text again matches the doc word for word.
14. **Open [i] icon in dark mode:** use the accent's lighter shade (indigo-400 for League) on a 15% tint; light mode unchanged.
15. **The tap test gets two modes** (`test/browser/tap_t.js`, documented in `test/README.md` and CLAUDE.md's checks):
    - **Full:** every target, on the tabs a build changes, and on every tab whenever `ui.js` hover or touch code changes.
    - **Sample:** first, last and one middle target per chart, on every other tab, every build.
    - Choose by argument (e.g. `npm run tap -- --full race`, `npm run tap -- --sample`); poll until the card changes, no fixed waits; hard timeouts; never leave processes running in the background.
    - For this build: full on Points, sample everywhere else.

## 4. Rules that apply

- **Hover cards on touch:** the shared `ui.js` helpers only; open on a tap, switch on another tap, close on a tap anywhere else or on scroll; one overlay at a time.
- **R14:** an [i] on every section; no description under section titles. **R4:** nothing under 11px.
- **Flags** drawn (`ui.js` Flag), never emoji. **Minus signs** U+2212. **Curly apostrophes** (’) in all visible text.
- **No typed-in counts:** 46, "8 games", "S1–S8" and every value come from the data.
- **Screen size:** `useScreen()` or Tailwind prefixes, never `window.innerWidth`. **Club-agnostic:** any save renders, including an empty one.

## 5. Must not change

- Points on desktop: layout, sizes, colours, the ledger (open, sortable, drawers), the hover card under the strip, a click on a cell opening its row (only the two subtitles move into the [i]).
- Points race on every screen: Focus / Compare / Pace, the season strip (All / None, focused season scrolled into view), the record pace line, the docked card and sideways scrub, full-width chart, bare matchday numbers on phones.
- Matchday benchmarks filters, stat cards, sorting, row drawers.
- Home vs Away apart from its apostrophes and the dark-mode [i] icon; every other tab and page.

## 6. Checks before the pull request

- **Test kit:** `check` (compile, syntax, render), `css`, `bundle`, `overflow`. Baseline (v51.19): 64 renders, 0 errors, 1,524 sub-11px hits, every League tab 390 wide. None may get worse.
- **Tap test:** full on Points (Focus, Compare, Pace, the 46 strip cells, the scrub); sample on every other tab; an [i] closing an open card.
- **Measured, results in the report:**
  1. Points at 390: the ledger is folded on load; the button reads "Show all 46 matchdays ▾" from the data; folding and unfolding work; no sideways page scroll.
  2. Scrub: a sideways slide across the strip produces at least 10 different cards; the page doesn't scroll sideways; an up-and-down swipe still scrolls.
  3. Card for three matchdays (one 100%, one 50–74%, one under 25%): record, win %, PPG, GD and best win equal that matchday's ledger row; the win % tag's colours as specified; the flag is drawn, not text.
  4. "See in ledger" for MD 23 and MD 44: the ledger unfolds, the row's centre is within 80px of the screen's centre, it is marked, its drawer is closed.
  5. Pace at 390: three control rows; the baseline switch fits one line; the legend shows the chosen baseline's value for each of the three choices.
  6. Points at 390 and 1440: no visible subtitle under either section title; each has an [i]; its text matches the blurbs doc word for word.
  7. Home vs Away: [i] text matches the blurbs doc word for word (curly apostrophes).
  8. Dark mode at 390 and 1440: the open [i] icon's contrast ratio against its tint, in the report.
  9. Desktop at 1440: before / after screenshots of Points, light mode; only the subtitles differ.
