# Canvas v51.19: Home vs Away on Phones + Section [i]

- **Date:** 2026-10-17
- **Goal (one line):** Home vs Away reads well on a phone, and the section [i] arrives (first on this tab).
- **Branch:** a new branch from an up-to-date main, e.g. `claude/v51-19-home-away`
- **Pull request title:** `Canvas v51.19: Home vs Away on phones + section [i]`

## 1. Context

This is the first build of the League phone pass, one tab per build (Home vs Away, then Points, then Spider Matrix). On a 390px phone the Venue ledger shows only its home half, and the side-by-side bars shrink to about 45px stubs. Both get phone layouts approved by the user on 17 Oct. The build also introduces the section [i] (rule R14): every section's description moves out of the layout into an [i] at the top right of its header. Home vs Away is the first tab to get it; Points and Spider follow in their own builds.

## 2. Scope

- **Files:** `src/ui.js` (the shared [i] piece), `src/LeagueTabs.js` (Home vs Away), `src/App.js` (version line), `CLAUDE.md` (two rules, section 7).
- **Tab:** League → Home vs Away only. Other tabs keep their descriptions until their own build.
- **Screen sizes:** phone 390 and desktop 1440, light and dark. "Phones" means widths under 640px.

## 3. What to build

No mock images; every item is in numbers below.

### 3a. Section [i] (new, shared, in `ui.js`)

1. **One shared piece** (e.g. `SectionInfo`) that any section header can take (e.g. an `info` prop on the existing section header). Built once, reused by every tab later.
2. **The icon:** lucide `Info`, 18px, colour slate-400. It sits at the top right of the section header, inside a 44 × 44px tap area, with a negative margin so the icon lines up with the card's padding edge. Where the header already has controls on the right (desktop), the [i] comes after them. While open: the icon takes the page accent colour on a 10%-tint rounded square (radius 10px).
3. **Desktop (640px and up): popover.** Opens under the icon, right edges aligned, width 330px, max height 60% of the viewport, scrolling inside. White card (dark-mode tokens in dark), 1px slate-300 border, radius 14px, shadow, padding 16px 18px. Eyebrow "About this section" (11px, 600, 0.1em, capitals, slate-500), then the text at 13px / 1.5, slate-700, bold runs in ink. Closes on outside click, Esc, or clicking the icon again.
4. **Phones: bottom sheet.** Use the existing `Sheet` in `ui.js`. Title = the section title (16px, 800); a 44px ✕; closes on ✕, swipe down or tap outside. Text at 14px / 1.5.
5. **One open at a time** across the page. The button has `aria-label` "About <section title>" and `aria-expanded`.
6. **Content format:** short rich text: a bold lead, paragraphs, bullet lists, bold labels ("How to use:", "Good to know:"). Keep each section's text next to that section's code, not in a central file.
7. **Home vs Away sections:** Venue swing, All seasons home and away, Venue ledger. Copy the text exactly from the "Home vs Away" part of the GSL Canvas: Section [i] Blurbs doc (https://claude.ai/code/artifact/7f1a307e-587e-4990-83b2-84692fb7d5b5). Drop the *(coming)* marker on the Venue ledger phone line, because this build delivers it.
8. **Remove the visible descriptions** under those three section titles (their `sub` text); the words now live in the [i]. No other tab changes.

### 3b. Home vs Away on phones (under 640px only; desktop unchanged)

9. **Venue ledger: one number a side, split bar in the middle.**
   - Header row: "⌂ Home" on the left in the home colour, "Season · <metric> split" centred (eyebrow style), "Away ✈" on the right in sky-600.
   - One row per season, at least 52px tall, divider on top. Left column 44px: the home value. Right column 44px, right-aligned: the away value. Middle: the season ("S1", 12px, 700) left and its division (12px, slate-500) right, then the split bar (10px tall, radius 5px, home colour then away colour, widths = the two shares), then the two shares under it (11px small-number font, slate-500), left and right.
   - The value is the metric picked in the Points / Wins / Goals switch: the same numbers and shares the desktop ledger uses for that metric.
   - Stronger side: value 16px, 800, ink; its bar segment full strength. Weaker side: value 600, slate-400; bar segment at 45% opacity. A tie: both 700, ink, both full.
   - Tapping a row still shows that season in Venue swing, as on desktop.
10. **All seasons, home and away (side-by-side): numbers raised, spine kept at 22px.**
    - Top line of each row: home value left (14px small-number font), the label centred (eyebrow style), away value right. Stronger side 800 ink; weaker side 600 slate-400.
    - Second line: the home bar grows leftwards from the spine; the spine (86px wide, no wrapping) holds the gap at 22px Outfit 800 with ◂ or ▸ pointing at the stronger venue, coloured home navy or sky-600; the away bar grows rightwards from the spine.
    - Bars: 8px tall, radius 4px. Each bar's length = its value ÷ the larger of the row's two values × its half-row width. Stronger bar full colour, weaker at 35% opacity.
    - Lower-is-better rows (draws, losses, conceded per game, failed to score) keep today's logic: the arrow points at the side with fewer.
    - Header line: "⌂ Home", the match count ("164 · 164 matches"), "Away ✈". Rows 9px top and bottom padding, divider between rows.
11. **Tap targets.** Every tappable thing on Home vs Away is at least 44 × 44px on phones: the metric switches, Combine seasons and its season chips, the swing chart's season bars, the ledger rows, the [i]. Small chips keep their look and get an invisible larger tap area; nothing may overlap a neighbour's tap area. List any control that couldn't get there.

## 4. Rules that apply

- **R14:** every section has an [i] top right; no description under section titles (this tab now; others on revisit).
- **R4:** nothing under 11px. **R7:** the odd Highlights card goes full width first (done in v51.18; keep).
- **Flags:** always drawn (`ui.js` Flag, or SvgFlag in charts), never emoji text.
- **Minus signs:** U+2212 through the shared formatter (v51.18).
- **Colour:** home navy and away sky stay as today; the faded side shows the weaker venue; numbers stay in ink.
- **Screen size:** `useScreen()` or Tailwind prefixes, never `window.innerWidth`. **Club-agnostic:** no club names; any save renders.

## 5. Must not change

- Home vs Away on desktop: layout, sizes, colours (only the three descriptions move into the [i]).
- Behaviour: the metric switches, select / combine seasons, the swing chart, sorting where it exists, tapping a ledger row to show it in Venue swing.
- Every other tab and page (their descriptions stay until their own build).
- v51.17–v51.18 fixes: dark-mode chips, Outfit titles, hidden Lab bar, minus signs, R7 order on phones, dark swing text.
